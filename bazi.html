八字 Bazi Chart

Four Pillars of Destiny — year, month, day and (optional) hour.

Day Master (日主) is outlined. Month pillar uses approximate solar-term dates (jie qi, e.g. Li Chun ≈ 4 Feb, Jing Zhe ≈ 6 Mar), so dates within a day of a boundary may differ from a precise almanac. Use local clock time; no true-solar-time or DST adjustment. For entertainment only.
